Connect this phone to Ember

  1. Open Ember on your computer.
  2. Press Ctrl+K and choose Connect your phone.
  3. Scan the QR code that appears.

The QR code is like a house key: anyone who scans it can see and type into your sessions. Only scan it on your own phone, and don't share screenshots of it.
